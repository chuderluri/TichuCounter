Tichu Counter replaces the paper score sheet for the card game Tichu. It
tracks the running score of the two teams and records the outcome of every
round.

Key features:

- Organise your players in groups; games, history and statistics are scoped
  to the active group.
- Compose two teams of two per game and swap players between rounds.
- Quick play: start an instant all-guest game and jump straight into scoring.
- Enter the score with a calculator-style keypad; the other team's score is
  complemented to 100 automatically.
- Record Small and Grand Tichu per player as made or lost, and double wins.
- Undo and redo every scoring action, even after restarting the app.
- Per-player statistics: games, wins, Tichu rate, average points and more.
- Fully offline: everything is stored locally on your device.
